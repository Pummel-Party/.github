## 🎮 Overview

**Pummel Party** is a competitive multiplayer party game built for players who enjoy chaotic mini-games, strategic board gameplay, and unpredictable social competition.

The game combines turn-based movement with real-time action mechanics, allowing participants to collect items, sabotage rivals, survive traps, and compete across a variety of challenges. It supports both local and online multiplayer sessions.

Players can use weapons, trigger hazards, interact with dynamic maps, steal advantages, complete objectives, and outplay opponents through tactical decisions and fast reactions.

With cartoon-style visuals, humorous destruction mechanics, multiple arenas, and varied mini-games, Pummel Party delivers energetic party gameplay for casual groups and competitive friends alike.
